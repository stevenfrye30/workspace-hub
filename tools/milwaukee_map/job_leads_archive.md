## City of Milwaukee — Vehicle Services Tech & City Laborer
- coords: 43.04171, -87.90975
- address: Milwaukee City Hall, 200, East Wells Street
- link: https://city.milwaukee.gov/jobs

✓ Verified Aug 10 — the pay is better than I said, but so is the bar. Vehicle Services Technician 3: $68K–$92K (employer-posted). Vacancies on both 1st and 2nd shift. Work is steering, suspension, HVAC, exhaust, emissions, transmission, fuel, brake, hydraulic, electrical and computer-controlled systems on cars through heavy trucks.
 
 Three catches: postings ask two years overhauling and repairing vehicles; you must supply your own standard industry tools; and it is DOT-regulated, so expect drug and alcohol testing including release of previous employers\u2019 test records from the past two years, plus random testing after hire. Mandatory emergency call-in.
 
 Easier doors at the same employer: City Laborer (DPW — streets, sewers, electrical services, sanitation, bridges &amp; buildings), HVAC Maintenance Technician 3 (police facilities), and Industrial Machine Repair Mechanic at Water Works — that last one posts continuously.
 
 Applications are online only at milwaukee.gov/JOBS, by 11:59pm on the deadline — there is no phone application route, which is why this pin carries no number. Postings are also pinned outside Room 706 at City Hall, weekdays 8:00am–4:45pm.
